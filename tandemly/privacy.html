Tandemly Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Tandemly is operated by Luxford Interactive Ltd (company number 17367736), a company registered in England and Wales with its registered office at 35 Spring Lane, Bottisham, Cambridge, CB25 9BL. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Luxford Interactive is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

2. Information you provide to us

This section, and Sections 3 to 9 below, describe the categories of personal data we collect directly from you and your partner as you use Tandemly.

3. Account and email information

Your email address and password credentials, used to create and access your account, plus your first name, date of birth and gender. We send email verification codes during sign-up and recovery codes when you ask to reset your password. Supabase provides authentication and stores the credential record; Tandemly does not store your password in its profile records.

4. Couple profile data

Your bio, prompt answers, and any partner details entered on your behalf if you set your Tandem up together in one sitting. A Tandem profile is shared between both partners in a couple.

5. Interests and preferences

The activity and lifestyle categories you select (for example, the things you’re both into), your couple composition, the couple compositions you both say you are open to meeting, and other discovery preferences such as age range and distance.

6. Potentially sensitive lifestyle and relationship information

Because Tandemly is a couples dating and lifestyle app, a couple-composition choice, meeting preference, bio, prompt answer, message, or statement about what someone is open to could reveal or allow someone to infer sexual orientation or other highly personal relationship and lifestyle information. Some of this may be special-category data under UK data protection law. Where that applies, we process it only as permitted by applicable data protection law. A person setting up or editing a shared profile must not provide sensitive information or choose shared meeting preferences without their partner's knowledge and permission.

7. Photos

Couple profile photos and images sent in conversations. These files are currently stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket and are delivered through public file URLs. The app limits where it displays chat images, but possession of a file URL may allow the file to be viewed without signing in. Do not upload an image that you are not comfortable sharing with its intended audience.

8. Approximate location

A location label (for example, a town or area) and, separately, more precise coordinates, which we use behind the scenes to calculate distance between couples. We only ever display an approximate area and a distance in miles to other couples – never your exact address or coordinates. You can hide your profile from Discovery entirely at any time in Settings.

9. Messages and interactions

The content of messages you send within Tandemly, plan suggestions, and your matching activity (who you’ve waved at, passed on, matched with, blocked or reported).

10. Matching and discovery

We use the information above – including your confirmed couple composition, the couple compositions you are open to meeting, activities and interests, location, and discovery preferences – to decide which couples to show you in Connect and Discover, and which couples are shown you. A new introduction is only eligible when each couple's confirmed composition falls within the other couple's stated preferences. Eligible couples are then filtered and scored using the remaining data. This is a straightforward rules-based process, not an opaque or automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

11. Reports and safety

If you report another couple, the app stores the selected reason, any details you provide, the reported couple and, while your account exists, your reporter identifier. Tandemly does not currently operate a continuously staffed moderation queue, so an in-app report must not be treated as an emergency service or a promise of immediate review.

12. Subscriptions and payment-related data

Tandemly Plus purchasing and restoration are not currently enabled, and the app does not currently receive subscription status from a billing provider. If subscriptions are enabled, this policy must be updated to identify the billing services and data actually used. Apple or Google would handle payment card details under their own privacy policies; Luxford Interactive does not intend to collect card details directly.

13. Device and technical data

Basic technical information needed to run the app, such as your device type, and a push notification token if you enable notifications. We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics trackers within the app.

14. Purposes of processing

We use your data to: create and run your account and Tandem profile; show your profile to, and show you, other couples; enable matching, messaging and plan-making; provide reporting and blocking features; provide customer support; and, if enabled in future, manage Tandemly Plus subscriptions.

15. Matching and recommendations

As described in Section 10, matching and any suggested activities are generated from the couple-composition choices, interests, location and preferences you’ve provided – we don’t buy in external data about you to power this.

16. Our legal bases for processing

Most account and service data is processed because it is necessary to provide the Service you request (performance of a contract), while limited technical, security and support processing may rely on our legitimate interests in operating and protecting Tandemly and assisting users. We may also process information where necessary to comply with a legal obligation. Where information described in Section 6 is special-category data, we process it only under a condition permitted by applicable data protection law.

17. Profile visibility

Your Tandem profile becomes eligible to appear to compatible couples on Connect and Discover once it’s complete – both partners have joined (or, in a together-mode setup, been fully entered), your profile meets the minimum content requirements, and your shared couple-composition preferences have been confirmed. You can hide your profile from Discovery entirely at any time from Settings, without deleting your account.

18. Processors and service providers

We currently use Supabase (database, authentication, Storage and server functions), Resend (sign-up verification, password recovery and support email delivery), Expo (app infrastructure, updates and notifications), and postcodes.io (postcode geocoding). Support messages send the category, reply email, message and, when available, a user identifier through a Supabase function to Resend and our support mailbox. Apple and Google distribute the app and may receive technical information through their platforms. RevenueCat is not currently integrated.

19. Sharing

We don’t sell your personal data. We share your Tandem profile with other couples using the Service, as that’s the whole point of Tandemly. We share data with the service providers in Section 18 as needed to run the app, and we may share information where we’re legally required to (for example, in response to a lawful request from a public authority) or to protect the safety of our users.

20. Retention

We keep account and profile records while an account is active. Account deletion removes the authentication user and profile records, but it does not currently delete messages already sent; those messages remain in the conversation with the sender identifier removed. The app has no user-facing message deletion feature. Uploaded profile and chat-image files may remain in Supabase Storage because account deletion does not currently sweep those files. Report records may remain without the deleted reporter identifier; when a reported couple is deleted, the report retains the original couple's identifier as an immutable historical reference for safety moderation. Support emails and provider logs follow the retention applied by the relevant mailbox or provider. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including safety, legal, support and operational requirements.

21. Deletion

You can delete your account permanently at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. This is irreversible and frees up your email address to sign up again as a new account. See Section 20 for the limits described above.

22. Security

Connections between the app and its online services use HTTPS/TLS, and Supabase database access policies restrict access to application records. Uploaded images are subject to the public-Storage limitation described in Section 7. The app also persists its authentication session in local application storage on your device. We review our technical and organisational security measures as the Service develops. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

23. Your rights under UK GDPR

If you’re in the UK, you have rights over your personal data under the UK GDPR, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your data, and the right to data portability where applicable. Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe we haven’t handled your data properly.

24. Access, correction, deletion and objection

Most of your data – your bio, prompts, photos, interests, location and preferences – can be viewed and corrected directly in the app, in your Tandem profile and Settings. To exercise any of the rights in Section 23, including a full copy of your data or an objection to a specific use of it, contact us at admin@luxfordinteractive.com and we’ll respond as required by law.

25. Age restriction

Tandemly is for adults aged 18 and over only. We ask for dates of birth and rely on users entering them accurately; we do not currently carry out identity, document or independent age verification. The primary account date is checked in the app and database, while a partner date entered during together-mode setup is currently checked by the app rather than a separate database constraint. If we receive credible information that an account represents someone under 18, we may investigate and restrict or remove it, but there is not currently a continuously staffed moderation process.

26. International transfers

Some service providers in Section 18 may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we use the transfer mechanism required by applicable data protection law for the relevant provider and destination, which may include an adequacy decision, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another lawful safeguard.

27. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the Service changes or as we act on legal advice. We’ll update the “Last updated” date above when we do. Significant changes will be flagged in the app where reasonably possible.

28. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to admin@luxfordinteractive.com or by post to Luxford Interactive Ltd, 35 Spring Lane, Bottisham, Cambridge, CB25 9BL.
