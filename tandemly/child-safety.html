Tandemly Child Safety Standards

Final Last updated: 2 September 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service Contact us

1. Zero tolerance

Tandemly, operated by Luxford Interactive Ltd (company number 17367736), has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Tandemly is an adult couples-dating and lifestyle app, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to hold an account.

2. Age requirement

Every account must declare a date of birth at signup, and Tandemly enforces an 18+ minimum age at the point of account creation. This age check is a required self-declaration; Tandemly does not currently perform government ID verification of age.

3. Google Play age-signal install restriction

In addition to Tandemly's own age gate, Tandemly has opted in to Google Play's own age-signal-based install restriction, which blocks installation of the app for users Google identifies as underage based on their Google account information.

4. In-app reporting

Every couple profile and every conversation in Tandemly can be reported or blocked. The in-app report form includes a dedicated “Suspected under-18 user” category, alongside other safety categories such as harassment, coercion, threatening behaviour, impersonation, and non-consensual images. Reporting and blocking do not require the reported couple to be told who reported them.

5. Review and enforcement

Reports are reviewed manually by Luxford Interactive staff. An account or couple found to be in violation of this policy, including any suspected under-18 use, is removed from Tandemly.

6. Reporting to authorities

Where Luxford Interactive identifies apparent child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in connection with Tandemly, it is reported to the appropriate authority — such as the UK's National Crime Agency (including CEOP) or the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) — as required by law.

7. Point of contact

The designated point of contact for Tandemly's child safety standards and compliance is admin@luxfordinteractive.com.
