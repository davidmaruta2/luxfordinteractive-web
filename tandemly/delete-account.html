Delete your Tandemly account or data

Last updated: 1 September 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

If you still have Tandemly installed, this is the fastest way and takes effect immediately:

Open Tandemly → Settings → Account → Delete account. You'll be asked to type “DELETE” to confirm. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Tandemly installed, email admin@luxfordinteractive.com from the same email address your account was registered with, stating that you want your Tandemly account and data deleted. We will action your request and confirm back to you by email, normally within a few business days.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data. From within the app, at any time:

Photos – remove individual couple photos from Settings → Edit profile → Photos. Takes effect immediately.

Bio and prompt answers – edit or clear these from Settings → Edit profile. Takes effect immediately.

If you'd rather request this without opening the app, email admin@luxfordinteractive.com from your account's registered address, stating what you'd like removed, and we will action it and confirm back to you, normally within a few business days.

One limit worth knowing: messages you've already sent to a match can't currently be individually deleted while your account stays active - Tandemly doesn't yet have a user-facing message deletion feature. See "What may be retained" below.

What gets deleted (full account deletion)

Your login, your profile (name, date of birth, gender, bio, prompt answers), your couple record if you were its only remaining member, your discovery preferences, and your device push token, if set.

What may be retained

Messages you have already sent to a match remain visible to the other couple in that conversation, with your sender identifier removed - Tandemly does not currently have a user-facing message deletion feature. Uploaded profile and chat-image files may remain in storage, since account deletion does not currently sweep those files. If you have submitted a safety report, the report record may be retained without your identifier attached, for safety and moderation purposes. Support emails and provider logs follow the retention applied by the relevant mailbox or provider.

See our Privacy Policy (Sections 20 and 21) for the full, current retention and deletion detail.
