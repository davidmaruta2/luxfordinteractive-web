The shift

AI has changed what expert builders can deliver.

For years, businesses were pushed towards expensive commercial software even when it only half-fit the job. Helpdesk platforms, CRMs, HR systems, finance tools, governance trackers, portals and internal workflows were bought because custom software sounded slow, risky or out of reach.

That assumption is now out of date. With the right tool stack and a disciplined delivery process, many of those systems can be replicated, improved and tailored for far less than traditional software budgets. The point is not novelty. The point is fit.

We do not build client applications by blindly handing prompts to vibe-coding platforms such as Replit Agent or Lovable. We use AI inside a professional developer workspace: source-controlled, reviewed, tested and owned by people who understand the code.

Local business support

Serious digital tools should not require enterprise budgets.

We prioritise practical, affordable software for small businesses serving their local communities. If your organisation has outgrown spreadsheets, inboxes and manual workarounds, we can help you shape a digital toolset that fits without paying for oversized platforms.

Our background includes NHS and ICB experience, so we have a particular interest in work that supports health, care and community services. But the principle is broader: useful applications should reflect the way real people work, communicate and keep things moving day to day.
