Values

Our ethos.

Every product we build is measured against these principles.

Opportunity

We care about building applications that create opportunity: from supporting young people into employment and career, to helping people access what they need without needing more money.

Learning

Life is all about learning, and pursuing a shared goal alongside another person is one of the best ways to do it. Bringing people together to grow through shared experience sits at the heart of what we build.

Real-world connection

Perhaps paradoxically for a software company, we care about ensuring digital leads to non-digital: a real conversation, a real meeting, a real moment offline.

Our stack

We know our stuff.

Every product we build, our own or for a partner, is engineered to the same standard: security and scalability from day one, one codebase across every platform, and a flexible database built for real integration. Where AI genuinely helps, we use it thoughtfully, not as a headline feature.

Waddl model

Trust is the product, not a feature.

01

Two simple lists

Users build "What I can offer" and "What I need" lists across everyday categories such as tutoring, gardening, website help, cooking, music lessons, DIY, and admin.

02

Direct swaps or points

A user can swap directly, or earn points helping one person and spend them with someone else. Points are banded by skill level rather than treated as a flat hourly credit.

03

Narrow MVP scope

The first scope stays in lower-risk home, lifestyle, tutoring, creative, tech, fitness, cooking, moving, errands, and car-cleaning categories.
